### About

I study how small-model agents learn from experience: how teacher trajectories can be reused during reinforcement learning, how agents should be evaluated in real service conversations, and how vision-language agents can reason about change in Earth-observation imagery. I also build the tools I use for reading papers and developing research ideas.

Outside research, I do strength training, cycling, and badminton.

### Research

**[Prefix-GRPO](https://github.com/HappynessI/Prefix_GRPO)** · From Trajectories to Prefixes: Reusing Teacher Trajectories via Replayed Prefixes and Online Continuation  
Splits a teacher rollout into replayable prefixes, restores each intermediate state by environment replay, and trains the student on online continuations from those states, optimizing both the replayed prefix tokens and the continuation tokens. Experiments on TextCraft, BabyAI, and ALFWorld; datasets and training metrics are released.

**[HMBB Benchmark](https://github.com/HappynessI/HMBB_Benchmark)** · Evaluating customer-support agents  
A 60-question benchmark built from real support conversations, scored with a six-dimension rubric covering business points, handling decisions, customer burden, tone, grounding, and internal support. Compares eleven combinations of system prompt, skill, and knowledge base.

### Research Tools

**[League of Idea](https://github.com/HappynessI/League_of_Idea)** · A literature-grounded workspace that turns a research direction into reviewable ideas through paper cards, gap hypotheses, critique, and revision, then ranks shortlisted ideas in an Elo arena.

**[MinerU PDF to Markdown](https://github.com/HappynessI/MinderU-pdf-to-md)** · A Codex skill that converts papers to structured Markdown with MinerU and translates them while keeping equations, tables, and figure links intact.

**[Remote Sensing Knowledge Base](https://github.com/HappynessI/RS_Knowledge_Base)** · An Obsidian-compatible research library on remote sensing change understanding, vision-language models, and agents.
